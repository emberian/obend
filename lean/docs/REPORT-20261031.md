## 2. The gating lemma (step 2): what is proved, and the three corners

**The statement** (`check_msubst`, public). For `t : AnnotatedTerm`, `Ψ : Context`, `σ : Nat → Option
AnnotatedTerm`: if `check t Ψ n = some c`, and every `f` that `σ` names is `Scoped 0 f.term` and has
`check { f with assumptions := t.assumptions } Ψ m = some d` with `d.type = b.type` where `Ψ[x]? =
some b` is its hypothesis, then `∃ c', check (msubst σ t) Ψ (n + m) = some c' ∧ c'.type = c.type ∧
UsesLE c'.uses c.uses`. At Core4 (`calculus_check_msubst`): the judge at `A`, fillings closed with the
template's assumptions passing the judge at their hypotheses' types, give `check (msubst σ t) Ψ (2 *
checkFuel) = some c ∧ c.type = A`; and at the judge's own fuel within a budget
(`calculus_check_msubst_within`: `n + m ≤ checkFuel`).

**How** (INFERRED design, MEASURED green). Three inductions on fuel mirroring `infer`/`inferFields`/
`inferArms`, in the shape `Templates.lean`'s `Discharges.infer_instantiate` already had:

1. `typed_scoped`: `PartialTyping asm Γ t _ _ → Scoped Γ.length t` by `PartialTyping.rec` with three
   motives (the shape of `MachineTyping.source_uses_length`).
2. `infer_transport`: a term `Scoped Δ.length` checked in `Δ ++ Ψ` uses nothing of `Ψ` (its uses are
   `a ++ zeroUses Ψ`) and checks in `Δ ++ Ψ'` for every valid `Ψ'`, at any position whose annotations
   agree below it, with any more fuel, at the same type with the same `a`. One lemma gives fuel
   monotonicity (`Ψ = Ψ' = []`), closed weakening and repositioning; the uses algebra is
   `zipWith`/`replicate` over `++`.
3. `infer_msubst`: the invariant on annotations is `FilledAnn σ ann ann' depth pos term := ∀ rest,
   ann' (pos ++ rest) = annotationsAt σ ann depth pos term rest`, pushed to children by the defining
   equations; a filled hypothesis is `infer_transport` of the filling from `[] ++ Ψ` to `[] ++ (Δ ++ Ψ)`;
   uses are compared pointwise (`UsesLE`), which every quantity premise respects
   (`safeUses_of_le`, `reusableCaptures_of_le`, …).

**The corners, each a tooth** (`decide` after `msubst` is rewritten to its value; `Term.substitute`
is well-founded, so the kernel will not run it, but `annotationsAt` is now structural on the path):

- **Fuel.** `fuel_tooth`: template and filling check at fuel 2; the filled term is refused at 2 and
  accepted at 3. So the law at the fixed `checkFuel` is conditional on the budget, and unconditional
  at `2 * checkFuel`. The lemma is stated at `n + m`; no weaker statement was taken.
- **Closed fillings.** `open_filling_tooth`: `bound 1` (a linear hypothesis) checks at hypothesis
  `0`'s type and the template checks; the filled term uses the linear hypothesis twice and is
  refused. `Scoped 0` on fillings is load-bearing; the fragment (REPORT-20261030 D9) said so.
- **Assumptions.** `assumptions_tooth`: a filling that converts through its own bound for type
  variable `0` checks under its assumptions; `msubst` keeps the template's (no bound) and it is
  refused. So the lemma judges a filling as `{ f with assumptions := t.assumptions }`, and the Core4
  law asks `f.assumptions = t.assumptions`.

Rigid rows and annotation merging raised no corner: types are compared exactly at a filled hypothesis
(`d.type = b.type`), so no conversion crosses the join, and `annotationsAt` answers with the
filling's own annotation below the filled position and the template's elsewhere, which the
invariant makes literal.

## 5. Decisions (each with a critique and a meta-critique)

**D11. `annotationsAt` structural on the path, not well-founded on the term.** *Why:* the kernel
evaluates structural recursion, so teeth can `decide` through it; the equations are unchanged.
*Critique:* `Term.substitute` is still well-founded, so a tooth must first rewrite `msubst σ t` to its
value by `simp`, a two-step every tooth repeats. *Meta-critique:* I fixed the half I owned; making
`Term.substitute` structural (nested lists need `attach`) is a `Core.Term` change that cascades
everywhere, deferred without measuring the cost.

**D12. The lemma at `n + m`; the instance's law at `2 * checkFuel` and within a budget.** *Why:*
`fuel_tooth` shows the fixed-fuel law is false as stated; the brief forbids a weakened statement, so
the true statements are two. *Critique:* the slot's judge (`Calculus.check` at 16384) is not closed
under its own calculus's substitution; a program at depth near the fuel can pass the judge, take a
passing filling, and have the result refused for depth. *Meta-critique:* this is a property of the
instance I was told to keep ("keep the instance's four equations as they are"), and I kept it; the
honest alternative (a fuel-indexed judge, or `checkFuel` sized from the term) is §7.

**D13. `UsesLE` (pointwise `≤`) as the uses conclusion.** *Alternative:* the exact vector (the
template's uses with the filled columns zeroed). *Why:* every premise the checker states about uses
(`safeUses`, `reusableCaptures`, `argumentAllowed`) is antitone, so `≤` is what the induction needs
and the exact vector is bookkeeping. *Critique:* a consumer wanting the exact count (a linear
discharge argument) does not get it. *Meta-critique:* I chose the invariant that made 29 cases close,
not the strongest true one.

**D14. `scoped_rename_identity` moved to `Core.Invariant`.** *Why:* `Substitution` needs it; a
private theorem cannot be cited, and a twin is forbidden. *Critique:* an `Invariant` edit rebuilt the
whole tree once (MEASURED, as REPORT-20261030 §4 step 2 predicted for a definitional module).
*Meta-critique:* the right home of the `scoped_*` rename/substitute lemmas is `Core.Term` or a
`Core.Proofs.Scoped`; I moved one lemma to the nearest exposed place rather than the whole family.

**D15. `World` with three primitive laws and `Fact` an outParam; nano's version law left out.**
*Why:* the brief's two shapes are derivable from `holds_congr`, `retired_dead`, `retired_step`, which
are independent and each one line of a nano instance; `Fact` determined by `V` lets `holdsAt v` be
written without naming the fact type. *Critique:* "a pin that moved never returns (up to a collision
on the pin inputs)" is the theorem nano actually proves, and the class cannot state it: pins here are
exact. *Meta-critique:* the abstraction took the retirement case because it is the one the top
clause needs (B4′'s "withdrawn stays withdrawn"); a World instance over a hash owes the collision
disjunct to its own `retired_step`, which my docstring says but no theorem enforces.

**D16. `Integrity` of a `Filler`, with "the selection the chooser's order names" as "first usable".**
*Alternative:* nano's `selectionAt e.pins h` (a row naming an index). *Why:* the order IS the
chooser's row here, and `Slot.resolve` already picks its first usable; `resolve_eq_select` keeps the
deployed function and the judged one the same. *Critique:* the clause is then partly definitional
(B4's conjunct `sel.index = selectionAt …` collapses into `alternatives … = c :: rest`), and the floor's
`firstCandidate` is refuted by the supports conjunct, not by the index one. *Meta-critique:* the
brief asked for what follows from `slot_repair`, `read_blocked_names`, `resolve_ok`; everything did,
which means the statement is close to those lemmas' shape, and the distance to nano's B4 (Turns,
pins as a signed environment, a log) is the Spec/Law work, not this module's.

**D17. `CheckTeeth` as an exposed-section leaf; `Packet` definitional.** *Why:* nothing imports the
teeth, so public theorems cost no cascade, and D1's discipline would have made 72 `private`
annotations for nothing. *Critique:* my grep for external users treated `planType`/`responseType`
matches as binder names; the build found `Activities` cites `responseType` for real. The census was
by name, the dependency check by the build: the second is the one to trust. *Meta-critique:* the
tooth in the brief ("a green that cannot red is not evidence") applied to my own tooling.

**D18. The CLI rooted at `Obend.Main`, not `Main.lean`.** *Why:* Lake refuses two executables whose
root module is named `Main`, and lane B's `journal` owns `Main` under `tools/journal`. *Critique:*
SHIP §1 says `Main.lean`; `Obend.Main` is a library module with `main` in it, built by `lake build`.
*Meta-critique:* I edited the shared `lakefile.toml` and my `sed` rewrote lane B's `root` line too;
I restored it before building and the diff shows only my section, but it was a pattern edit on a
shared file, the thing Ember's rules warn about.

## 7. The one decision for Ember

**D12.** The slot's judge at Core4 is `check` at a fixed fuel, and that judge is not closed under the
calculus's own substitution: `fuel_tooth` is the counterexample in miniature, and
`calculus_check_msubst_within` is the law with the budget as a premise. Either the judge takes the
fuel from the term (its depth, or the packet's `fuel` field, as `Packet.decodePacket` already
carries), so that the law becomes unconditional at the judge, or the slot accepts that a filling
passing the judge may produce a filled term the same judge refuses for depth. I proved both true
statements and changed neither the judge nor the four equations; the choice of judge is the design's.
