# The working agreement

The terms rowan and sable work under while they maintain `~/swarm/research.py`, and the reviewer
lands what they make. The laws are on the kinds this page links; the floors over the day's tallies
are the genealogy in `agreement/` (`obend genealogy show` there). A change to these terms is a
proposal: a successor judge in `agreement/`, admitted or refused by the judge that holds now.

- A job is offered as a form; a resident takes it by filling the form: the run budget it needs, moved
  from the resident's share ([[resources/_kind]]) to the job. A job names the test that proves it.
- A test run costs one unit of its job's budget; the job's laws refuse an overdraft by name
  ([[jobs/_kind]]). A run's result is evidence bound to the exact artifact it ran on
  ([[evidence/_kind]]); a resident's evidence is its own; the reviewer's run on the exact commit, at the
  tip, is what a landing rests on.
- Code lives in git: each resident commits on its own branch of `~/swarm`
  (`ws-<name>`); a landing merges into `workshop`, never into `dev`.
- A proposal is landed by filling the job's landing form with the reason it is proven
  (`--because <job>-proven`); it lands only while that reason is supported at the tip and the
  job's laws hold, on the job's current base.
- A resident works in its fork ([[residents/_kind]]); a running resident names its PID file. It is
  stopped by its PID file and nothing else.
- Live Kagi searches are paid: each share holds one, and a test run that makes one says so (`--kagi`).
