# Ambition

Objective Bend is being built to become the symbolic core of what comes after Mathematica: a
language and kernel in which mathematics, programs, documents, contracts and the people and agents
who work on them live in one world, where every answer carries the evidence a stranger can check,
every refusal names what would pass, and the system is as automated as the laws of computer science
permit. This file says what that means and what it does not.

## The claim

A term rewriter with a notebook was the twentieth century's symbolic core. What it lacks is not
more rules. It lacks objects many hands build without forking; laws judged at the moment someone
tries to change something; provenance that survives a second author; answers labeled by the regime
they were established in (decided, proved by a certificate someone can re-check, or merely
attested); deferred decisions that stay open with their justifications until a chooser settles
them; and a kernel that never trusts a search it did not run. Objective Bend has these in a
mechanized metatheory. Its calculus is small on purpose: Faré's objects (a layer sees the whole it
will be part of and the thing it extends, and `fix` closes the stack lazily), laws by occasion,
slots with withdrawable supports, a guarded runner, documents with pushout merge. Everything rich
sits beside it as judged subsystems and native leaves, never inside it.

## The destinations

- **A symbolic workspace.** Expressions are objects whose terms are data, rewrites are certificates
  from a rewrite judge, and a notebook is a document whose cells are slots; a simplification with
  no certificate is attested and the receipt says so.
- **Staged, reflective metaprogramming that keeps its properties.** Quotation at the bottom of an
  observer ladder, splicing as checked contextual substitution, reflection as an attenuable
  capability, stages as views of the world, specialization as a candidate in a slot whose supports
  are the pins it assumed, so deoptimization is the withdrawal of a support and the policy theory of
  the compiler is already proved.
- **A runtime that makes the partial-evaluation JITs weep** because every assumption it specializes
  on is a support, every optimization is a candidate with a justification under a cost relation, and
  the receipt says who assumed what. One intermediate language; the lazy demand machine as the
  always-usable fallback; affine strict leaves where the parallelism lives; a leaf compiler we own.
- **Automation as powerful as the laws permit.** Solver diversity is architectural: SAT, SMT,
  pseudo-Boolean, ASP, computer algebra, model checkers, provers, proof search, language models, all
  as producers of certificates that one registry of judges checks, each with its trust base and its
  regime named in the receipt. Power comes from the breadth of the registry and the cheapness of
  its checkers, never from trusting a cleverer producer.
- **Platforms as application kinds over the same world:** smart contracts where a receipt is a
  proof and the shared-World ledger is the chain; metadata-driven business software where catalogs,
  documents and registers are kinds with laws, posting is a turn, period closing is a version law,
  reports are judged folds with non-omission receipts, and offline work merges through one door;
  distributed widgets whose view is a method, whose events are plans and whose affordances are the
  requests the judge says would pass.

## What we hold to

The kernel admits by re-execution and checks certificates it never searched for. Every judgment
carries its quantifier and its date. Resources are not meanings: budgets, ticks and tokens are
declared and can fail by name. A law is a line a person can read, judged on every write,
inheritable, and amendable by someone who did not write it. Nothing lands beside; everything
replaces. One stranger-usable artifact stays ahead of every platform. A generalization is adopted
only when it shortens proofs against a control, and a refuted one is kept as the record of its
refutation. We refuse higher structure that does not pay for itself, circuits in the trusted base
until the fold is proved, and uniform nouns that erase different semantics.

## What this is not

Not a bigger calculus: Core4 does not grow a CAS, a verb catalog or a view IR. Not a stack of
trusted interpreters: the tower is a sequence of worlds with the same kernel, and a reflective
change is a judged turn. Not a solver: the system is as automated as the certificates it can check.
Not a catalog of demos: the measure is a person's completed workflow on the shared world, including
restart and repair.
