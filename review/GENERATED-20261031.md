## 0. The finding in one paragraph, and the inventory

INFERRED. Ember's question was: HoTT studies what is generated by constructors and equations; what
are *our* generated things, and which of their equations are which? The answer the library gives,
once its objects are listed with their equations, is that it is a **set-level library on purpose**:
for terms, objects, worlds and justifications every equation is a computation rule (a definitional
equality the kernel evaluates), and the few equations that are *paths* (propositional, needing a
witness the kernel checks rather than computes) sit in exactly three places: the machine against
the reference (`Chain`), equi-recursive type agreement (a bisimulation), and the patch groupoid of
pages. That is not an accident: admission by re-execution *is* the policy "every equality the
admitter needs is definitional", and the arithmetization question is "which of those definitional
equalities would a stranger rather receive as a path with a witness". The epistemic reading then
says what a witness teaches whom. So the three readings are one ladder read at three heights, and
the places where each is real rather than a metaphor are the same three places.

Two corrections to the brief's phrasing, carried through the readings. INFERRED. (a) There is no
"history groupoid": a world's steps are audited and forward-only (`retired_step`: a tombstone is
terminal; `ver_step`: a version is kept or strictly `Past`), so the history is a category (a
preorder on views, `Later`) and only the *patch* layer has inverses. (b) A conflict is not "a witness
that a square does not commute": in the thin category of pages every square commutes; a conflict is
the pushout existing among pages and not among files (`files_no_pushout` against `merge_antichain`),
the obstruction to a section of `File ↪ Graph`. The genuinely non-commuting case is `no_cocone`
(two pages storing different content at one identity), which content addressing forbids
(`compatible_of_addressed`).

## 1. The HoTT reading

### 1.1 What the reading would say

READ (Angiuli–Morehouse–Licata–Harper 2014, `~/paperbin/angiuli-…-homotopical-patch-theory.txt`
§1, §3, §6). A *patch theory* is a higher inductive type: repository contexts are points, patches
are paths, patch laws are paths between paths; identity, inverse and composition "are provided by
the higher inductive type and automatically satisfy the groupoid laws"; a *model* is a function out
of the type into `Type`, functorial for free; merge is derived from a pseudocommutation of adjacent
patches, which needs inverses. On higher structure they are explicit (`:1036–1052`): nothing forces
two different proofs of a patch law to be equal, so one may either keep proof-relevant patch laws
or truncate the repository type to a 1-type, "an appropriate and helpful modification" for their
example. Their future work (`:1460–1470`): patches that are partial need "a directed homotopy type
theory, with, for example, a universe of partial bijections", which they did not have.

INFERRED. HoTT's division of equations is: a *definitional* equality is a computation rule the
type checker runs (`fix` unfolds, `β` reduces); a *propositional* equality is a path, a term the
checker checks. The reading of our inventory is therefore a sorting: which equations in the table
are computed and which are witnessed, and whether any witnessed equation has paths between its
witnesses that matter.

### 1.2 Each kind of thing, sorted

**Terms and objects (`Core/Term.lean`).** READ. Every equation is a `Step`; `Step` is deterministic
(`Law/Run.step?` is a function and `step?_*_cong` are its congruences); the object's defining
equation `fix spec s = spec (fix spec s) s` is `Step.fix`, a computation rule. INFERRED. The reading
makes exact why `SYNTHESIS` §8.11 was right to refuse Cook–Palsberg's domain theory as kernel
structure: in HoTT terms the object is the *syntactic* fixed point, and `fix_unfold` is definitional;
a cpo least fixed point would be a *propositional* equation (an equality in a domain, witnessed by
continuity), which the library needs only for `≈b`. The only paths at this level are: `Chain` between
the lazy machine state and the reference (a bisimulation-up-to-resources, witnessed by the forcing
chain, `Core/Proofs/Forcing/Chain.lean`), and equi-recursive agreement of types, where
`FiniteBisimulation` *finds* a finite relation and the kernel *checks* it is a bisimulation.
PROPOSED, instance: equi-recursive type equality is the coinductive equality of M-types, and
"bisimilar implies equal" is the coinduction principle HoTT proves for M-types (Ahrens–Capriotti–
Spadotti 2015, from memory). The checker's search for the finite witness is the constructive content
of that principle; `equirec_reduce` without the transparency escape (`3e1d7c8`) is the kernel
refusing to let the witness be a definitional unfolding, which is exactly the HoTT line between the
two kinds of equality.

**Specifications.** READ. `mix` composes by `mixBody`; `Spec/Contract.run_append` is the contract of
a composition. INFERRED. The composite's associativity is an equality *up to β* of `mixBody` nests:
a path whose witness is a `Steps` derivation, not a computation. The cost-sensitive associativity
(sharing) is a path in a different type (improvement), as `METATHEORY.md` §2 places it. No higher
structure: paths between two β-derivations are irrelevant to anything the library states.

**Plans and the tree (`Core/Interaction.lean`).** READ. `Node` has six constructors and the tree is
`observe b : State → List Term → Node`, "the node reached after each finite sequence of responses";
`spin` is a node of its own, "ignoring internal steps never equates spinning forever with eventually
yielding"; `node_of_chain_vis` says a machine yield is the reference `Vis` for every state in a chain
with it, and for every response the resumed checkpoint is in a chain with `k r`. INFERRED. The tree
is a coinductive object (an M-type: the final coalgebra of `Node`-shaped trees over responses), and
`observe b` is the anamorphism from states. The equation the module *promises* and does not have,
`observe_of_chain : Chain s t → ∀ rs, observe b s rs = observe b t rs`, is the statement "a
bisimulation implies equality of unfoldings", the coinduction principle again; the module's
`endsAbove_unique` is the lemma that makes the anamorphism well defined without resources. Xia et
al.'s `eutt` (READ `~/paperbin/xia2020-interaction-trees.txt`, from memory for the definition:
weak bisimulation up to finite `Tau`) is refused, and should stay refused, for the reason the
docstring gives: the library quotients *resources* (`EndsAbove`) and never *steps*, so `spin` stays
distinguishable from a long yield. This is the second place the HoTT reading is real: the tree is
the one object whose equality is coinductive, and the theorem it makes statable is one the library
already owes itself.

**Worlds (`Slot/World.lean`, `Run/Spec.lean`).** READ. `Step` is audited and directed;
`retired_step` and `ver_step` make tombstones and versions forward-only; `Past` is a strict order
(`past_irrefl`, `past_trans`); `worldOf v₀ log` is a fold; `refines` says a reachable configuration's
world, spent tokens, plan and history *are* the folds of its log (`Inv.world`, `Inv.spent`,
`Inv.obj`). INFERRED. The brief asks whether "admission by re-execution" is "a definitional equality
between two derivations of the same world". It is, literally: two nodes holding the same log compute
`worldOf v₀ log` and agree by `rfl`; the propositional half is `refines`, proved once, saying the
executable runner's state is that fold. In HoTT terms the world is 0-truncated (a set), has no path
constructors, and has a canonicity theorem (`refines`) instead; two derivations of the same world are
equal because there is only one. That is not a limitation; it is the design that makes re-execution
admission sound (nano `CHARTER.md:26`: "Admission is re-execution; the proof slot stays empty"). The
path-shaped things at the world level are directed: the version law `ver_behind_forever` ("a version
state that moved never returns") says the history category has no loops on versions; the
`Collides` disjunct of `stale_behind_forever` says pin-equality reflects that order only up to a
collision of `pinOf`, and `Exact` is the injective case. Angiuli's own remark applies: paths in HoTT
are symmetric, and a directed theory is what a world would need. So the HoTT reading of worlds is a
metaphor, and the right non-metaphor is in §3: a Kripke frame with persistent propositions
(`Permanent` is exactly "false is upward-closed along `later`").

**Patches and pages (`Doc/Patch.lean`, `Doc/Merge.lean`).** READ. `apply_compose`, `apply_invert`
(under `Effective`), `invert_invert`, `step_dual`; `Same` is equality of the three observations;
`Le` is a preorder, hom-sets are subsingletons; `merge_isPushout`, `pushout_unique`, `no_cocone`,
`files_no_pushout`. INFERRED. This is the one place the reading is real at level 1 and literal:
effective patches between pages-up-to-`Same` form a groupoid (identity `[]`, composition `++`,
inverse `invert`), and Angiuli's "provided by the HIT" groupoid laws are our three theorems. Their
2-paths (patch laws) are, for us, *propositions*: `(G.apply p).Same (G.apply q)`, because `Graph`
with `Same` is a set, so our model is 0-truncated and the patch theory is automatically 1-truncated,
the modification Angiuli call "appropriate and helpful". Their example 2-path constructor "edits to
independent lines commute" (`:827, :886, :950`) becomes a theorem we do not yet have:

```
commute_disjoint : Disjoint (touches p) (touches q) →
  (G.apply (p ++ q)).Same (G.apply (q ++ p))
```

and their merge-by-pseudocommutation becomes, on disjoint spans, Mimram's pushout:

```
merge_of_disjoint : p.forward → q.forward → Disjoint (touches p) (touches q) →
  (merge (G.apply p) (G.apply q)).Same (G.apply (p ++ q))
```

With stable identities pseudocommutation is the literal commutation of names, which is why
`SYNTHESIS` §8.4 found "no reordering of patches is ever computed": none needs to be. Where the two
accounts diverge is exactly `Effective`: two compatible patches that both add the same
content-addressed atom have a pushout (`merge`) but `p ++ q` is not effective (the second `add` is
not), so the groupoid is a proper sub-structure of the thin category. The 2-cells never carry data:
Angiuli's open question about proof-relevant patch laws has, for this model, the answer "no, by
construction".

**Repair (`Slot/Repair.lean`).** READ. A repair is an audited world step establishing a fact;
`repair_sound` under `Compatible` (the repair preserves every requirement of the candidate that
already holds); Astra's clash countermodel (`clash_unsat`) is two incompatible repairs. INFERRED.
"A repair is a path that stays inside the fibre of usable candidates" is accurate as one sentence:
the base is views under `Later`, the fibre over `v` is `{c | usable (holdsAt v) c}`, `repair_sound`
is a transport lemma and `Compatible` is the condition under which transport exists. It is not a
fibration (no lifting property, no unique lifts); `SYNTHESIS` §8.2 said the same. Keep the sentence,
refuse the word.

**Justifications (`Just.lean`, `Slot.lean`).** READ. `Just K` is a bounded distributive lattice;
`Poly X` is the antichain normal form; `canonical`: two antichains with the same Boolean function
have the same monomials; the dual evaluation `missing` names what failed. INFERRED. A lattice is a
(0,1)-category: no paths, nothing to witness; `canonical` is the extensionality principle for
justifications (equal as functions, equal as data), the level-0 shadow of univalence. Where
proof-relevance would enter is one notch up, in Green–Karvounarakis–Tannen's `N[X]` (counting
derivations, READ `~/paperbin/green-karvounarakis-tannen-provenance-semirings-pods2007.txt`),
which the project refuses (`SYNTHESIS` §8.6, "we never count"). The reading places the three as a
truncation ladder, `N[X] → Poly X → Bool`: all derivations, the minimal supports by name, one bit.
That ladder is not decoration; it is the disclosure dial of §3 seen from the justification's side.

**Receipts.** INFERRED. A receipt is a morphism of the history category with its witness carried
(for a page, a path in the groupoid, invertible; for a world step, directed, with no inverse), and
"a compact proof of this history" is a composite with its decomposition elided, which is what
breadstuffs' recursive aggregation does (`light_client_verifies_whole_history`,
`CONSTRUCTIVE-KNOWLEDGE.md` §6). HoTT adds nothing here beyond the word "composition".

### 1.3 Where the reading makes a theorem an instance, and where it is a metaphor

Instances (INFERRED): `apply_compose`/`apply_invert`/`invert_invert` are the groupoid laws Angiuli
get from the HIT; `merge_isPushout` and `pushout_unique` are Mimram's by design; `files_no_pushout`
is Mimram's diagram (3); equi-recursive agreement is M-type coinduction; `refines` is a canonicity
theorem (worlds are a set); `canonical` is extensionality for justifications.

Metaphors (INFERRED): "a patch is a path between worlds" (a patch is a path between *pages*; a world
step is audited, directed and may retire); "conflict = non-commuting square" (§0(b)); "history
groupoid" (§0(a)); "fibre of usable candidates" beyond the one sentence.

Does Angiuli's higher structure extend to worlds, plans and justifications? No, and for three
different reasons: worlds are directed (the symmetric path structure is wrong, not merely absent);
plans and trees are coinductive, where the right content is the coinduction principle, level 1; and
justifications are a lattice, level 0. For pages it is present and 1-truncated by `Same`. The
reading stops at the first level everywhere, and for worlds it does not reach the first.

### 1.4 Cost to adopt, and the experiment

Cost (INFERRED). As vocabulary for `Doc` and `Interaction`: one structure of thirty lines and two
theorems. As the library's organizing account: wrong, because the organizing fact of the library is
that its equations are computed, and an account built around paths would invite inverses where the
kernel's whole point is that there are none (`retired_step`, `ver_step`, `OnceSpent`).

Experiment, one day (PROPOSED), `lean/Obend/Doc/Groupoid.lean`:

- `EffPatch G H := { p : Patch // Patch.Effective G p ∧ (G.apply p).Same H }`, with `id`, `comp`
  (from `effective_append`, `apply_compose`), `inv` (from `apply_invert`), the groupoid laws up to
  extensional equality of patches (`∀ G, Effective G p → (G.apply p).Same (G.apply q)`), each
  discharged by the three existing theorems and nothing new;
- `commute_disjoint` and `merge_of_disjoint` as above;
- teeth: `base`'s `connect 1 2` twice (compatible, pushout exists, composite not effective), and
  Mimram's diagram (3) (`acb`, `adb`: disjoint, so `merge_of_disjoint` applies and the composite
  carries the antichain `{c, d}`; the conflict is in the composite, not in a failed commutation).

Pass: Angiuli's merge-by-commutation and Mimram's pushout coincide on disjoint spans from the three
existing theorems plus `apply_frame`-style per-atom reasoning. Fail: `commute_disjoint` needs more
than disjoint `touches` (an `add … after a` whose `a` another patch tombstones, say), in which case
the groupoid is not the thin category's substructure and the reading is decorative for `Doc` too.
The separately owed theorem `observe_of_chain` is not an experiment; it is a docstring's promise.

## 2. The arithmetization reading

### 2.1 The trade, stated in §1's terms

READ. nano: "Admission is re-execution; the proof slot stays empty; no circuit, STARK, folding
scheme or accumulator is built here or enters the TCB" (`CHARTER.md:26–27`); one writer per World,
followers re-derive every turn (`:31`). breadstuffs: a turn's STARK is a knowledge-proof and the
light client cannot be fooled: from `verifyBatch vk pi π = accept` alone, `∃ pre post, StateDecodeC
… ∧ kstep pi.effect pre post` (`CONSTRUCTIVE-KNOWLEDGE.md` §6, `lightclient_unfoolable_free`), with
the honest caveat that existence is proved and uniqueness of the state behind a root is open.
obend today: re-execution throughout. The front end admits only a byte-identical core
(`GROUNDING.md` §2 `admitted_front_end`; `Spec/Identity`, `Spec/Capture`); the gate re-runs
`Bidirectional.checkAgainst` (`Elab/Gate.lean`); a law is judged by running the machine on a probe
(`Law/Machine.judge`, `judge_sound` under any policy and resources, `judge_complete` with enough);
the runner decides `admitOp` at the current view and `reachable_legal` says the log is `Legal`.

INFERRED. In §1's sorting, re-execution is "the admitter evaluates the definitional equality
itself"; proof-carrying admission is "the admitter checks a propositional witness of the same
equality". The only reason to move an equality from the first column to the second is the asymmetry
breadstuffs builds on (`CONSTRUCTIVE-KNOWLEDGE.md` §0): checking is cheap and trusted, search is
expensive and untrusted, and a verifier may lack the time, the vault or the right to re-execute. So
the arithmetization question for obend is not "circuits or not" (the charters have answered that)
but "which of our definitional equalities already have the shape of a checkable witness", because
that shape is also what a receipt is.

### 2.2 What the circuit languages after snarky learned about that shape

From memory unless marked. snarky (o1Labs): a circuit is a `Checked` monad computation that emits
R1CS constraints; `exists` with an `As_prover` block computes the witness; the author writes two
programs (witness computation, constraint emission) and is responsible for their agreement. The
lessons since, as I read them:

1. **Under-constraint is the bug class.** READ (`~/paperbin/audit-blog-trailofbits-axiom-halo2-circuits.txt:49`):
   "one of the most common and severe bug classes for ZKP circuits is known as 'under-constrained'
   soundness bugs"; Halo2's `assign_advice` creates no constraint while `assign_advice_from_constant`
   does (`:55`). READ (Veridise, circom-pairing, `:229–235`): the verifier "must not accept more
   input-output pairs than anticipated", and end-to-end testing cannot find this. INFERRED. The
   theorem a circuit needs is therefore *accept ⊆ spec*, and the way to get it is to derive witness
   and constraints from one source with the refinement proved once: breadstuffs' `EffectSpec2 →
   derived circuit → derived apex`, "proven ONCE; ~29 effects are thin instances"
   (`docs/LOGIC-COMPILER-ASSESSMENT.md` TL;DR), and its `#arithmetizeTypedPredicate` emitting a
   descriptor and kernel-checked theorems in one command (`Dregg2/Crypto/Arith/ArithmetizeTypedPredicate.lean`).
2. **Bind the whole statement.** READ (Frozen Heart, `~/paperbin/audit-blog-trailofbits-frozen-heart-plonk.txt:41`):
   Fiat–Shamir challenges computed without the public inputs broke Dusk's plonk, SnarkJS and gnark.
   INFERRED. In receipt terms: the challenge, and so the receipt, must bind every public coordinate
   of the claim (pre root, post root, law identity, cited pins); breadstuffs' anti-ghost property
   (§5) is the same requirement at the state level.
3. **Hint, then constrain.** Noir's `unconstrained` and Leo's witness functions are snarky's
   `As_prover` made explicit: compute off-circuit, check in-circuit (division as `q·b + r = a`). The
   checker already works this way: the annotated term is the hint, `checkAt` the constraint.
4. **A VM, or a circuit per program.** READ (title only, `~/paperbin/avigad-verified-algebraic-representation-cairo-cpp2022-2109.14534.pdf`):
   Cairo is one CPU AIR with a verified algebraic encoding; the program is data. Lurk (audit in
   paperbin; design from memory) puts a Lisp evaluator in-circuit over content-addressed
   expressions, so a term's identity is a field element and substitution is pointer manipulation.
   INFERRED. These are the two routes for our two gates: the law judge is VM-shaped, the checker is
   certificate-shaped.
5. **Tables.** From memory (Lasso and Jolt are in paperbin, `lasso-2023-1216.txt`,
   `jolt-2023-1217.txt`, not re-read here): lookup arguments make "is this row in that table" the
   cheap primitive, and a VM whose instructions are table lookups is simpler than one whose
   instructions are gates. INFERRED. Everything in obend
   that is a finite relation checked by membership (`labelsDistinct`, `lookupV`'s visited walk,
   a bisimulation, a nullifier set) is table-shaped.
6. **Fold the history.** Nova-style IVC (from memory) proves `n` applications of a step function by
   accumulating instances; breadstuffs' `light_client_verifies_whole_history` is the same fold at the
   turn level. INFERRED. Anything in obend stated as `AtEach` over a `foldl` is IVC-shaped.

breadstuffs' own measured caution (READ, `LOGIC-COMPILER-ASSESSMENT.md`, header): the compiler
spine exists, but 65 of 91 deployed descriptors are hand-written, and the hash floor the apex needed
(`Poseidon2SpongeCR`) is false at the deployed field (`CONSTRUCTIVE-KNOWLEDGE.md` §6). The
arithmetization reading must not pretend the last mile is a formality.

### 2.3 Arithmetizing obend's three admission gates

**The bidirectional checker (`Core/Bidirectional.lean`, `Core/Check.lean`, `Elab/Gate.lean`).**
READ. `synthAt`/`checkAt` "recurse on the term (no fuel)" and "carry the declarative derivation
(`PartialTyping`), so soundness is by construction"; conversion happens only at the mode switch,
`whnf` and joins; `checkWork` measures the work against the packet's grant; `BidirectionalCompleteness`
gives annotation witnesses for the whole declarative grammar. INFERRED, circuit-shaped as a
certificate checker: the derivation is the witness; each `PartialTyping` rule is a local relation
between a node's row and its children's rows (hashes of term and types as cells, `Core/Digest` as
the content address, Lurk's lesson); `checkWork` is the trace length, so the grant is the row count
and the budget refusal is "trace too long". The two non-local parts are conversion and
equi-recursive agreement, and both are table-shaped: the prover supplies the finite bisimulation
`R` that `FiniteBisimulation` finds; the constraints are "every pair the derivation converts across
is in `R`" and "`R` is closed under one productive unfolding" (each row's successor pairs are rows).
That is a lookup argument, item 5. The theorem the circuit would need is `checked_erasure` plus the
soundness of `PartialTyping`; the prover-completeness it would need is `BidirectionalCompleteness`.
`agree_never_manufactures_shareability` is a sample of the "no extra accepted pairs" obligations
item 1 demands.

**The law judge (`Law/Machine.lean`, `Core/Machine.lean`, `Core/Checkpoint.lean`).** READ. A kernel
runs the probe `case (law old new req) [ok ↦ true, refused r ↦ asLabel r]` on the machine; `judge_sound`
holds under any policy and resources and `judge_complete` with enough; `execute_ground_iff`: a
successful execution extracts the observation exactly when the term evaluates to it; the machine
state is "first-order Lean data (heap of cells holding closures, control, frame stack)" and
`Checkpoint` encodes a state "as a canonical token list" with a proved round trip. INFERRED,
VM-shaped: one row per machine transition, the `Checkpoint` encoding is already the row format,
the heap of cells is memory (read/write consistency by a permutation argument), the law and the
request are public inputs by digest, the verdict is one public output; resources (`Limits`,
`Budget`) are trace bounds, and `judge_complete`'s "enough resources" is prover-completeness. The
soundness statement a circuit inherits is exactly `judge_sound`'s shape: *accept ⇒ `Judged` over
`Evaluates`*. The certifying shape of `keeps` (`guardedBy`, `Law/Claim.lean`) is syntax-directed
and total, so an installed claim is a certificate already. The hard part is what Lurk found hard:
substitution and closures; the heap machine, not `Law/Run.step?`, is the thing to arithmetize.

**`Legal` on an event log (`Run/Spec.lean`).** READ. `worldOf`, `spentBy`, `histOf`, `endedOf`,
`planOf`, `citesOf` are `foldl`s; every clause is `AtEach`, "a property of every position, of the
prefix before it and the event there"; `Inv` records that the configuration's `world`, `spent` and
`obj` are those folds; `OnceSpent` is `Nodup`; `Authorized` and `DecisionsHeld` read
`host.view (worldOf v₀ pre)`. INFERRED, IVC-shaped: the accumulator is `Inv`'s recomputed
configuration, each event a row, each clause a transition constraint on (accumulator, row);
`OnceSpent` is the nullifier set (a multiset non-membership, item 5); `Paired`/`AfterAbandon` are a
two-state automaton over the accumulator's `inflight` and `ended`; the one external read is the
world's view, which must be witnessed. Here the World's own law does the work: `holds_congr` says a
support's truth is a function of the pin its cell holds, and `read_congr` says two views agreeing
on the pins of a candidate's supports read it the same, so the witness for a row's view reads is
the list of `(cell, pin)` openings for the facts it cites, and nothing else of the world. That is
the pin-sufficiency theorem, and it already exists. `ver_step`/`Past` supply the forward-only
epoch that replay protection needs. Nothing in `Legal` resists the fold.

**What is not circuit-shaped.** INFERRED. Search: the chooser's order into `Slot.resolve`, and
which repair to take (`Slot/Repair.lean`: "the planner's"); these are inputs, and a circuit takes
them as such. Coinduction: `spin` and `EndsAbove` quantify over all resource vectors; a prover can
show "ended with this node under this budget", never "never halts"; breadstuffs'
`OptimisticAdjudication` is the honest account of claims decided by an absence. Hash evaluation
inside the kernel (`Core/Digest`: one Keccak permutation is outside any proof budget), and in a
circuit the choice of an arithmetic-friendly hash whose collision resistance is not refuted at the
deployed field. The adversarial host (`Host.allowed` is "whatever the host decides",
`REPORT-RUNNER` §5), which no witness constrains.

### 2.4 What a receipt becomes, and the dial

PROPOSED. A receipt is a compact witness of a morphism in the history category:

```
{ pre post : Root;  event : Event;  law : Digest (Spec/Identity-style);
  pins : List (Cell × Option Pin)  -- the openings the row's view reads cite
  verdict : Verdict;  π : Option Proof }
```

For re-execution admission `π` is empty and the stranger needs the vault (`host.view`). With the
openings and a computable reading of pins, the stranger needs the pins only. With a proof, one bit
and the roots. The library's existing receipts are already at the middle position: `Reading.usable
c ev` lists the supports with their truth (the openings by name) and `Reading.blocked c d` lists the
dead ones (`read_blocked_names`, `missing_mono_mem`): a refusal is a selective-disclosure receipt by
construction, which is the why-not completeness of `Integrity.contextual`.

The one refinement the receipt needs (PROPOSED): a computable pin semantics,

```
holdsOfPin : Cell → Option Pin → Fact → Bool
holds_pin  : holdsAt v f = holdsOfPin (cell f) (pin v (cell f)) f
```

after which `holds_congr` is `rfl`. In §1's terms this moves a World law from the propositional
column to the definitional one, and that move is exactly what lets a stranger *compute* the read
from pins rather than be told it. `binds_or_collides` (`Core/Digest.lean`) is the binding the pins
then carry.

Does the disclosure dial give a stranger a receipt they can check without the vault? READ
(`Metatheory/EpistemicDial.lean`): the dial is a chain `acceptanceOnly < selective < fullDisclosure`,
acceptance is invariant under the dial (`accepts_invariant_under_dial`), and leakage is monotone
(`leak_mono`). INFERRED. Yes at `selective`, for the facts the stranger is allowed to see: the pins
and openings of the cited supports replace the vault, by `read_congr` plus the `holdsOfPin`
refinement. The stranger learns those facts by name, which is the point of a refusal and the
problem of a confidential one (§3.4). At `acceptanceOnly` the stranger learns one bit, which needs
the breadstuffs lineage the charters keep out of the TCB. At `fullDisclosure` the receipt is the
log and the stranger is a follower.

### 2.5 Instances, new theorems, cost

Instances (INFERRED): `judge_sound` / `judge_complete` are circuit soundness and prover
completeness; `refines` is breadstuffs' `integrity_guarantee` shape (post-state = fold of the
trace) and `apply` is the same for pages; `read_congr` is pin-sufficiency of witnesses; `OnceSpent`
is a nullifier set; `Checkpoint`'s round trip is a canonical row encoding; `execute_ground_iff` is
"accept iff evaluates"; `binds_or_collides` is the binding a receipt needs.

New theorems (PROPOSED): `legal_iff_fold` (§2.6); `holds_pin` making `holds_congr` definitional;
`receipt_sufficient`: a stranger holding the cited pins recomputes the read; `checker_rows`: a
`PartialTyping` derivation is a list of rows with a local constraint per rule, and the list checks
iff the derivation holds; `bisim_table`: `FiniteBisimulation`'s output is a table closed under one
unfolding.

Cost (INFERRED). As circuits: refused by both charters and by breadstuffs' own measurements; do not
pick a field or a hash, do not put `spin`-shaped claims in a receipt. As a *shape*: free, because
it is already true, and worth stating: every admission is a fold with an accumulator and witnessed
oracle reads; every receipt is the accumulator's roots plus the openings a row cites. Adopting the
shape costs one module and buys the receipt type the brief asks about and the interface Astra's
composition experiment needs (§3.5).

## 3. The epistemic reading

### 3.1 The vocabulary

READ. breadstuffs §0: "a principal's authority over a resource is exactly its ability to
constructively exhibit a witness the kernel accepts"; authority is *not* affine descent; four
facets: non-forgeability, monotone attenuation, kernel enforcement, zk-checkability.
`Metatheory/ConstructiveKnowledge.lean`: `Claim`, `Holds`, `Knower`, `holds_iff_discharged_witness`,
`verifier_learns_only_acceptance`. `Metatheory/EpistemicConsensus.lean`: a Kripke `Frame Ω ι` with
`Knows i φ w` and `DistKnows B φ w`; "a `Verify`-discharged statement is distributed knowledge among
the honest agents"; `honest_dist_knowledge_iff_holds`; a discriminating model where the group's
distributed knowledge strictly exceeds any member's. `CommonSecret`: threshold distributed knowledge
`D_G^{≥K}`, the dual of common knowledge. `OptimisticAdjudication`: a verdict upheld by the absence
of a refutation within a window is a third regime, sound only under `Actuated`, and the floor is
false without it. Halpern–Moses 1990 (`~/paperbin/halpern-moses1990-…txt`): Proposition 4, whenever
the generals attack it is common knowledge that they are attacking; Theorem 5, with unreliable
communication `C_G φ` holds in a run iff it holds in the run where no message arrived; Theorem 11,
with unbounded delays the same for ε-common knowledge ("asynchronous communication channels are of
no use for coordinating actions that are guaranteed to be performed at all sites within a
predetermined fixed time bound"); Theorem 12, the weaker variants `C^T`, `C^ε`, `C^◇`.

### 3.2 obend's objects, read as knowledge

**A support is a claim the World discharges; a token is what cannot be re-exhibited.** READ.
`Slot.supports c : List Fact`, `holdsAt v f`, `Spec.requires` (reusable facts, "used, never
consumed") and `Spec.consumes` (one affine token), `admitOp = requires.all holds && live token`.
INFERRED. The brief's "a slot's support as a claim some member can witness" is half right: the
member *cites*; the World *answers* (`Host.view`). The CK reading sorts `admitOp` exactly: `requires`
is the knowledge half (a Claim whose Verify is `holdsAt`, re-exhibitable every time, "authority does
not deplete by being used"), `consumes` is the non-forgeability half (`OnceSpent`), and breadstuffs'
warning that authority is not affine descent is obend's design: facts are reusable, tokens are not.

**`read_congr` is distributed knowledge, literally.** READ. `read_congr`: two views agreeing on the
pins of a candidate's supports read it the same; `holds_congr`: a support's truth is a function of
its cell's pin. PROPOSED, an instance: take the frame whose worlds are views, with agent `i`'s
indistinguishability `v ∼ᵢ v' := pin v cᵢ = pin v' cᵢ` for the cell `i` holds; then for
`G = {holders of the pins of S.supports c}`, `DistKnows G (fun v => S.read (holdsAt v) (some c) = r) v`
is `read_congr` restated, and the discriminating model is a candidate citing two cells: the holder
of one pin does not know the read, the pair does. This is the single most literal instance any of
the three readings produces: not "like" distributed knowledge, but the definition unfolded.

**Withdrawal makes knowledge dated.** READ. `Permanent holdsAt later f := ∀ v v', later v v' →
holdsAt v f = false → holdsAt v' f = false`; `retired_later`; `ver_behind_forever`;
`blocked_forever`, `stale_blocked_forever`; `DecisionsHeld`: "every dispatched call's decision held
at its dispatch view". INFERRED. `Permanent` is Kripke persistence of a negation: knowing a support
is dead is stable along `later`. What is *not* stable is "usable"; so what a receipt can ever
certify is `usable at v`, a timestamped proposition, which is Halpern–Moses's `C^T` rather than
`C`. breadstuffs' `settlement_soundness` ("a settled turn exercised an authority live at the
settlement tip", §7) is `DecisionsHeld ∧ Authorized` at `viewAt`: knowledge-live-at-dispatch. The
version law's `Collides` disjunct is the epistemic honesty of pins: a stranger knows a version moved
only up to a collision of `pinOf`, never better.

**"Cannot be fooled", with several of everything.** READ. `refines`: a reachable configuration is its
log's folds; `reachable_legal`. INFERRED, one runner: a verifier holding the log and `host.view`
recomputes the configuration, so "cannot be fooled" is re-execution, definitional (§1). Several
members of one World: the log-holders have `D_G` of everything the log says
(`honest_dist_knowledge_composes` is the composition), and no member alone has more than the pins
it holds. Several runners sharing a World: Astra's finding (`ASTRA-EFFECT-LAWS` §1.2) that
"re-execution gives agreement about a judgment on the same input; it does not by itself serialize
competing successes" is, in this reading, Halpern–Moses Proposition 4 and Theorem 5: a unique grant
is a *coordinated action*, so it requires common knowledge of the grant, so it requires a
simultaneous event, and with unreliable or unboundedly delayed communication no exchange of
messages creates one. nano gets its simultaneity by fiat, one writer per World (`CHARTER.md:31`):
the writer's log is the common clock, and followers re-deriving it is how `C_G` is attained.
Several runners must re-introduce that point of simultaneity as a shared atomic step, which is
Astra's design B and its experiment (`§9`); the reading says that step is not an implementation
detail but the only thing that can make `C_G(granted)` true. Breadstuffs' optimistic regime
(upheld by silence in a window) is Theorem 11's weaker variant in witness form:
`optimistic_unsound_without_actuation` is "asynchronous channels are of no use for fixed-bound
coordination", proved as a floor.

**Withdrawable justifications and the stranger.** INFERRED. A receipt dated `v` teaches "usable at
`v`"; "usable now" needs current pins; `blocked_forever` says a retired support never comes back,
so a dated receipt citing one is known-stale forever (persistent). The dial's selective position
names the cited facts (`evidence`) and a refusal names the dead ones (`missing_mono_mem`): a
refusal leaks, by design. Astra C's "refusal disclosure" worry (§4, "confidential diagnostic data
cannot be dumped into a public refusal") and `Integrity.contextual`'s why-not completeness are in
direct tension, and the tension is a decision, not a theorem: a confidential law must trade the
refusal's names for a coarser reason, and `Poly`'s normal form is where the coarsening would be
specified (which tokens a stranger may be shown).

### 3.3 Is this the right logic for distributed properties? Where it meets Astra

INFERRED. Epistemic logic is the right logic for three things and the wrong logic for three others.
Right: what a receipt teaches whom (`D_G` over pin-holders, the dial); what coordination requires
(`C_G ⇒` simultaneity `⇒` a shared step or a single writer); how withdrawal behaves (persistence,
dated knowledge). Wrong: the joint invariant itself (ticket exclusion is a safety property of a
global trace, Astra B's rely–guarantee, not a knowledge formula); liveness; and hyperproperties in
general, of which knowledge formulas are a fragment (Finkbeiner's survey is in paperbin and Astra
§4 cites it for exactly this; the knowledge fragment is from memory, not re-read here).

Where the two studies meet (READ `astra/ASTRA-EFFECT-LAWS-20261031.md`; INFERRED the meetings):

- **B and Halpern–Moses.** Astra's "atomic shared transition wrapper" and the pass criterion that
  "the atomicity premise does observable work" is exactly Proposition 4's prerequisite made
  mechanical. The epistemic reading supplies the *reason* B must be built first: no amount of local
  `Legal` can produce common knowledge, so `local_legal_does_not_imply_global_once` is a theorem of
  the frame, not a bug of the runner. Astra's `AuthorizedHolding` versus `BelievesHolding` is
  `holds` versus `Knows i holds`.
- **C and Kripke frames.** Astra's two-run hyperlaw ("samePublicInputs ∧ sameSchedule ⇒
  samePublicReceipts") is `¬ Knows stranger (private)` in the frame whose worlds are admitted logs
  and whose `∼_stranger` is equality of the public observation map; breadstuffs' PerfectZK
  (`view_indep_of_witness`) is the same statement at the acceptance position. So the epistemic frame
  is the semantics Astra C needs for its "observation discipline", and C's checkable fragment is
  the `K`-formulas over it. The subset-closure fragility Astra notes (§4, "inherited narrowing
  becomes delicate") is the known fact that `∀∃` knowledge formulas are not preserved by removing
  worlds.
- **D and persistence.** Astra's withdrawable attestations with "immutable evidence IDs and a
  revision parent" are `Permanent` plus the version law: a withdrawn token is dead forever by name,
  and the receipt is dated. Doyle's TMS, which Astra cites, is what `blocked_forever` already is.
- **A and the dial.** A plan contract's receipt "should name whether it certifies the operation,
  its current residual, or the entire installed activity": three positions of what the receipt
  discloses about the tree, the dial applied to plans.

## 4. Verdicts

**The reading I find most real: the arithmetization reading, taken as a shape and not as
circuits.** It adds no vocabulary: `Legal`, `apply`, `worldOf`, `judge` and `checkAt` already are
folds with accumulators and witnessed reads, `read_congr` already is pin-sufficiency, and
`judge_sound` already has the soundness shape a witness would inherit. It also answers Ember's
question most directly: what we generate are set-level objects whose equations are computed, and a
receipt is the accumulator's roots plus the openings a row cites. The single most literal instance
among all three readings is epistemic (`read_congr` is `DistKnows` unfolded); the HoTT reading is
real for pages (level 1, truncated by `Same`) and for the tree (coinduction, with `observe_of_chain`
owed), and a metaphor for worlds and justifications.

**Refusals, plainly.** HoTT for worlds (directed; inverses would be a bug) and for justifications
(level 0); `eutt`; cpo fixed points as kernel structure (`SYNTHESIS` §8.11 stands); circuits in the
TCB (both charters stand; breadstuffs' own numbers say the last mile is not free); epistemic logic
as the logic of joint invariants or liveness; the phrases "history groupoid" and "conflict as a
non-commuting square" (§0); any receipt that claims `spin`.

**The experiment I would run first: `Run/Fold.lean`, `legal_iff_fold` with per-row reads (§2.6).**
It is the cheapest to refute, it decides what a receipt is, it turns `read_congr` into a receipt's
size, and it hands Astra's shared-World composition experiment the interface it needs (the global
wrapper is a fold over interleaved logs with a shared `spent` accumulator). Second,
`Doc/Groupoid.lean` (§1.4); third, `Slot/Knows.lean` (§3.5), which should be run after the
`holdsOfPin` refinement so that its constructive tooth has something to bite.
