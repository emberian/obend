# Prior art by aspect: what the previous states of the art were, and the lessons we take

Written 2026-10-30 after a Kagi pass that pulled 38 papers into `~/paperbin` (filenames below,
each with a `.txt` sibling). Where I describe a paper's content from memory rather than from the
text in paperbin I say so. This is a map for the lanes, not a literature review.

## 1. The checker: bidirectional typing and its completeness

**State of the art.** Pierce and Turner's *Local Type Inference* (TOPLAS 2000,
`pierce-turner2000-local-type-inference`) set the shape: synthesis for eliminations and variables,
checking for introductions against an expected type, with the recognizable fragment characterized
syntactically. Dunfield and Krishnaswami's survey (CSUR 2021, `dunfield-krishnaswami2021-bidirectional-typing`)
is the reference for *annotatability*: a bidirectional system is complete for its declarative
system modulo annotations on β-redexes, and the survey lists the design rules (introduction forms
check, eliminations synthesize, the subsumption rule sits at the mode switch) that make completeness
provable by induction on the declarative derivation. Lennon-Bertrand's *Complete Bidirectional
Typing for the Calculus of Inductive Constructions* (ITP 2021, `lennon-bertrand2021-complete-bidirectional-typing-cic`)
is the closest precedent to our situation: a mechanized completeness proof for a dependent type
theory that hit unannotated lambdas, empty eliminations and conversion placement, and resolved them
exactly as Argon proposed for us. Abel, Öhman and Vezzosi (POPL 2018,
`abel-ohman-vezzosi2018-decidability-of-conversion`) show how to get a decision procedure for
conversion without fuel, by a logical-relations proof of normalization inside the type theory.

**Lessons.** Completeness is a theorem about a *fragment you name*, never about everything the
declarative relation accepts; the fragment is syntactic once conversion lives at the mode switch.
Fuel in a checker is a smell that a well-founded measure has not been found; Abel et al. remove it
by proving normalization first. Our three counterexamples are the textbook three.

## 2. Quantities: usage as a synthesized output

**State of the art.** McBride's *I Got Plenty o' Nuttin'* (2016, `mcbride2016-i-got-plenty-o-nuttin`)
and Atkey's *Syntax and Semantics of Quantitative Type Theory* (LICS 2018,
`atkey2018-syntax-and-semantics-of-qtt`) put grades on binders in dependent type theory with the
zero grade for erased terms; Idris 2 is their implementation. Wood and Atkey's *A Linear Algebra
Approach to Linear Metatheory* (ESOP 2022, `wood-atkey2022-linear-algebra-approach-linear-metatheory`)
is the paper for us: usage vectors over a semiring as *outputs*, metatheory by linear algebra,
mechanized in Agda. Granule (Orchard, Liepelt, Eades, ICFP 2019, `orchard-liepelt-eades2019-granule`)
is the graded-modal practice with a working checker; *Linear Haskell* (Bernardy et al., POPL 2018,
`bernardy2018-linear-haskell`) is the industrial retrofit, and its lesson is that linearity on
arrows rather than types kept backwards compatibility. Already in paperbin:
`gaboardi-katsumata-orchard2016-combining-effects-coeffects-grading`.

**Lessons.** Our `PartialTyping` already has the Wood–Atkey shape (uses synthesized bottom-up by
`addUses`), which is why no solver is needed for usage. BendTT's own choice, affinity only, with
`Data` as the one copyable kind, is a point in this space, and the comparison to Atkey's `0/1/ω`
semiring is the precise way to state what Bend gave up. Branch joins by sum versus by maximum is a
semiring choice (additive versus multiplicative treatment of control flow) that the literature
treats as a parameter, not a fact.

## 3. Rows, aliases and recursive types

**State of the art.** Amadio and Cardelli's *Subtyping Recursive Types* (TOPLAS 1993,
`amadio-cardelli1993-subtyping-recursive-types`) give the coinductive, visited-set decision
procedure for equi-recursive type equality and subtyping; Brandt and Henglein (1998) axiomatize it
coinductively (the open PDF I found was front matter only, so it is not in paperbin). Gaster and
Jones (1996, `gaster-jones1996-polymorphic-type-system-extensible-records-variants`) and Leijen's
*Extensible Records with Scoped Labels* (TFP 2005, `leijen2005-extensible-records-scoped-labels`)
are the two row disciplines: lacks-constraints versus scoped (duplicate-allowing, shadowing) labels.

**Lessons.** "One head unfolding" is not a known equality; Amadio–Cardelli's visited pairs are, and
they terminate without fuel because the set of variables is finite. Our duplicate-label behaviour
should pick Leijen's scoped semantics or Gaster–Jones's lacks constraints and state it once, shared
by the relation and the checker. Rigid template variables are F-bounded quantification at
elimination (lookup through the lower bound), not equality, which is already how our data treats them.

## 4. Objects: open recursion, mixins, linearization

**State of the art.** Reddy's *Objects as Closures* (1988, `reddy1988-objects-as-closures`) and
Kamin's *Inheritance in Smalltalk-80* (POPL 1988, `kamin1988-inheritance-in-smalltalk-80`) gave the
two denotational encodings Faré calls Y and U; Cook and Palsberg's *A Denotational Semantics of
Inheritance and its Correctness* (1989/1994, `cook-palsberg1994-denotational-semantics-of-inheritance`)
is the fixed-point-over-generators account whose equations Faré says were consequences, not axioms;
Bracha and Cook's *Mixin-Based Inheritance* (OOPSLA 1990, `bracha-cook1990-mixin-based-inheritance`)
named the mixin; Flatt, Krishnamurthi and Felleisen's *Classes and Mixins* (POPL 1998,
`flatt-krishnamurthi-felleisen1998-classes-and-mixins`) gave mixins a typed operational semantics
with a soundness proof. On linearization, Ducournau et al. (OOPSLA 1994,
`ducournau1994-monotonic-multiple-inheritance-linearization`) stated monotonicity, and Barrett et al.
(OOPSLA 1996, `barrett1996-monotonic-superclass-linearization-dylan`) gave C3, which Faré's C4
extends with the suffix property. Cook's *On Understanding Data Abstraction, Revisited* (OOPSLA 2009,
`cook2009-on-understanding-data-abstraction-revisited`) is the clearest statement of why objects are
not ADTs, which bears on why laws judged at the object are not theorems about a closed program.

**Lessons.** Everything in Core4's `mix`/`fix` has a 1988–1998 precedent with a correctness proof;
what is new in our setting is laziness as semantics, reflection with a stated observer table, and
the typing of templates. Flatt et al. is the model for how to state and prove type soundness for
mixins operationally; our `MODULAR-TYPING.md` should cite it. C3's monotonicity and local-precedence
constraints are the properties the ordered-presentation invariance theorem should restate.

## 5. Laziness: call-by-need and its equivalences

**State of the art.** Launchbury's *A Natural Semantics for Lazy Evaluation* (POPL 1993,
`launchbury1993-natural-semantics-lazy-evaluation`) is the reference heap semantics; Breitner's
Isabelle formalization of its adequacy (2014, `breitner2014-adequacy-of-launchburys-natural-semantics`)
is the mechanization to learn from; Sestoft's *Deriving a Lazy Abstract Machine* (JFP 1997,
`sestoft1997-deriving-a-lazy-abstract-machine`) derives the machine with update frames and
blackholing that our demand machine resembles; Ariola and Felleisen's *The Call-by-Need Lambda
Calculus* (JFP 1997, `ariola-felleisen1997-call-by-need-lambda-calculus`) gives the equational
theory without a heap. For equivalence with cost, Moran and Sands' *Improvement in a Lazy Context*
(POPL 1999, `moran-sands1999-improvement-in-a-lazy-context`) is the operational theory we want for
`≈b`, and Hackett and Hutton's *Call-by-Need is Clairvoyant Call-by-Value* (ICFP 2019,
`hackett-hutton2019-call-by-need-is-clairvoyant-call-by-value`) is the tool that makes cost
reasoning about laziness tractable without a heap.

**Lessons.** Mini's demand machine is Sestoft's machine with checkpoints; Breitner shows what an
adequacy proof against Launchbury costs in a prover; improvement theory is the equivalence that
respects the resource pricing the world does, and `≈b` on completed runs is weaker than what a
priced kernel needs. The clairvoyant view may let `mix` associativity be proved without the heap at all.

## 6. Modular syntax and metatheory

**State of the art.** Swierstra's *Data Types à la Carte* (JFP 2008, `swierstra2008-data-types-a-la-carte`)
for extensible syntax; Delaware, Oliveira and Schrijvers' *Meta-theory à la Carte* (POPL 2013,
`delaware2013-meta-theory-a-la-carte`) for modular soundness proofs over feature algebras in Coq;
Pyrosome (2025, `pyrosome2025-verified-compilation-modular-metatheory`) for verified compilation
over modular metatheory, which is the shape of our Leaf refinement.

**Lessons.** The hole exercise measured exactly what Delaware et al. predicted: generic traversal
lemmas survive a new constructor, domain lemmas (dispatch, encoding, machine correspondence) do not,
and no feature algebra removes the second class. Pyrosome is the precedent for stating a compiler
correctness theorem that survives adding a language feature, which Leaf will need.

## 7. Step-indexing and program logics

**State of the art.** Appel and McAllester's indexed model (TOPLAS 2001,
`appel-mcallester2001-indexed-model-recursive-types`) and Ahmed's step-indexed logical relations
(ESOP 2006, `ahmed2006-step-indexed-syntactic-logical-relations`) are where "the lemma holds at
n + m" comes from; Iris (Jung et al., already in paperbin as `jung2018-iris-from-the-ground-up`)
hides the indices behind the later modality; *Iris in Lean* (2026, `iris-in-lean2026`) is the port
whose core is complete and whose adequacy layer is in progress.

**Lessons.** D12 was step-indexing in a checker; the fix we took (meaning fuel-free, procedure
graded) is the standard move. For `≈b` over the heap, Iris-Lean is the tool, and its adequacy gap is
the one thing to check before committing a lane to it.

## 8. Incremental computation and from-scratch consistency

**State of the art.** Acar's thesis *Self-Adjusting Computation* (2005,
`acar2005-self-adjusting-computation-thesis`); Adapton (Hammer et al. 2014, in paperbin); Mokhov,
Mitchell and Peyton Jones' *Build Systems à la Carte* (2018, in paperbin), whose verifying traces
and "from-scratch consistency" are the names our journal and nano's `receive_congr` carry.

**Lessons.** Kimi's closure bug was an instance of the oldest mistake in this literature: a
dependency walk whose termination argument does not track visited nodes. The property to prove is
Mokhov's: a verifying trace is correct iff rebuilding from scratch agrees, which `mem_closure_iff`
now gives at the graph level and the UNAFFECTED-only-if theorem would give at the verdict level.

## 9. Holes, truth maintenance, and the slot

Already in paperbin from earlier lanes: Nanevski–Pfenning–Pientka's CMTT, Cave–Pientka's first-class
substitutions, Omar et al.'s Hazelnut Live, de Kleer's ATMS, Doyle's TMS (1979) and his 1992
foundations-versus-coherence essay, Elkan's reconstruction via stable models, Green–Karvounarakis–
Tannen and Grädel–Tannen on provenance semirings, Stein et al.'s demanded abstract interpretation,
Cai et al.'s theory of changes, de Alfaro–Henzinger's interface automata. New: Blinn et al.,
*Statically Contextualizing Large Language Models with Typed Holes* (OOPSLA 2024,
`blinn2024-statically-contextualizing-llms-typed-holes`), the one paper that already treats a hole's
typing context as the thing an LLM should be given.

**Lessons.** The slot is a TMS whose problem solver is a type checker (DESIGN-CTX said so); the
foundations-versus-coherence debate (Doyle 1992) is the design axis for how a withdrawn support is
handled, and our choice (foundations: supports are recorded reasons, withdrawal is by name) is
stated, which the literature says is the honest side to be on when reasons must be auditable.

## 10. Authority, capabilities, and refusals that explain

**State of the art.** Dennis and Van Horn (1966, `dennis-vanhorn1966-programming-semantics-multiprogrammed-computations`)
invented capabilities; Miller's *Robust Composition* (2006, in paperbin) is the object-capability
doctrine the kernels follow; Macaroons (Birgisson et al., NDSS 2014; only slides found open, not in
paperbin) and Garg–Pfenning's PCFS (in paperbin as `morgenstern2011-lpcfs`) are the two shapes of
attenuated, revocable, use-once authority, and nano's charter chose the macaroon trade (re-execute
the check) over the proof-carrying one.

**Lessons.** "A refusal names the clause and the request that would pass" has no direct precedent I
found; the nearest are Macaroons' caveat-by-caveat verification failures and Pierce–Turner's local
error messages. That may be a genuine contribution of this project and should be stated as such.

## 11. Patches, transclusion, documents

Already in paperbin: Mimram–Di Giusto's categorical theory of patches, Angiuli et al.'s homotopical
patch theory, Shapiro et al.'s CRDT survey, Kleppmann's JSON CRDT and AT Protocol papers. New: Nelson's
*Xanalogical Structure, Needed Now More than Ever* (CSUR 1999, `nelson1999-xanalogical-structure`),
the primary statement of transclusion and two-way links that breadstuffs' "Xanadu made honest" answers.

**Lessons.** The pushout is the right merge *when it exists*; Mimram–Di Giusto's free finite
cocompletion is how conflicts become objects rather than failures, which is the slot's
`alternatives` by another route. Nelson's deep links are our cites with byte commitments.

## 12. Affinity and `Type : Type`

**State of the art.** Cardelli's *A Polymorphic λ-calculus with Type:Type* (1986; no open PDF
found) proposed it for programming with no logic; Terui's *Light Affine Set Theory* (2004,
`terui2004-light-affine-set-theory`) is the consistency-via-no-contraction tradition BendTT belongs
to; Girard's *Locus Solum* is in paperbin. Ringer's thesis *Proof Repair* (2021,
`ringer2021-proof-repair-thesis`) completes the proof-repair set beside PUMPKIN Pi.

**Lessons.** BendTT's consistency argument is Terui's lineage made syntactic; the thesis
(`THESIS.md` §1) should cite that lineage rather than only Taelin's tweets, and say plainly that the
object layer sits above it rather than contradicting it.
