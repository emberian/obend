# Scholar brief: what are the things we are generating?

Ember, 2026-10-31, thinking out loud: "just like HoTT studies how things are generated by
constructors and equations, I kinda wonder about the things we're generating and studying, what
these plans are, what 'world objects' are; and I'm also thinking back to when we were working on
the snarky zk DSL in OCaml and what later zk/circuit-focused languages found since then, and to the
metatheory we did in breadstuffs and minidregg about arithmetization and distributed epistemic
logics." This is a study for the two of them to evaluate; it lands nothing in `lean/`.

The objects, as the library has them today. Terms: Core4's constructors (`lean/Obend/Core/Term.lean`),
with `mix`/`fix` and the reference `Steps`. Specifications as generators and objects as their fixed
points (Cook–Palsberg in `~/paperbin`, Faré's LTUO). Plans: what `perform` yields, a typed node with
a continuation; the whole behaviour is an interaction tree (`Core.Interaction`, Xia et al. in
paperbin). Worlds: a history of admitted events, re-derivable from pins (`Obend/Slot/World.lean`;
the runner's `Legal` in `Obend/Run/Spec.lean` is a predicate on event logs). Patches and pages:
`Obend/Doc` with merge as a pushout and conflicts as objects (Mimram–Di Giusto and Angiuli et al.'s
homotopical patch theory, both in paperbin). Justifications: supports as a lattice of eligibility
provenance (`Obend/Just.lean`, Green–Tannen). Receipts: minidregg's "what changed, from which root,
under which law; can I hand a compact proof of this history to another device" (`PROJECT.md`, read-only).
breadstuffs' metatheory, read-only at `/Users/ember/dev/breadstuffs/metatheory`: `CONSTRUCTIVE-KNOWLEDGE.md`
§5–6 (a receipt binds the whole post-state constructively; a turn's STARK is a knowledge-proof; the
light client cannot be fooled: `accept ⟹ ∃ pre post, a genuine kernel transition`),
`Metatheory/{ConstructiveKnowledge,EpistemicDial,CommonSecret,ResharingChain,OptimisticAdjudication}.lean`
(authority as constructive knowledge; the disclosure dial from full trace to one acceptance bit;
knowledge across adversaries), `Dregg2/Crypto/Arith/*.lean` (arithmetization of kernel steps into
gate constraints with the byte pin), `docs/LOGIC-COMPILER-ASSESSMENT.md`. paperbin also holds a zk
shelf: sumcheck and its verified variants, cp-SNARKs, the audit write-ups of Circom, Halo2, Plonk and
gnark bugs, Fong–Spivak, Baez–Courser's structured cospans, Jacobs's categorical logic, Lawvere.
Ember's snarky: o1Labs' OCaml DSL where a circuit is a monadic computation emitting R1CS constraints
(fetch its paper or README through Kagi if useful; key in `~/dev/allgame/.env`, request shape in
`~/dev/allgame/claude_resident/tools/web_tools.py`; `~/.annas-key` and `$ANNAS_SECRET_KEY` for papers,

common knowledge; whatever the circuit languages after snarky learned (Noir, Leo, Cairo, Lurk, the
Halo2 chip model, Nova-style folding, lookup arguments) about the shape of a constraint program and
its witness.

The question, in three parts. (1) The HoTT reading: for each kind of thing we generate (term,
specification, object, plan, tree, world, patch, page, justification, receipt), what are its
constructors, what are its equations, which equations are definitional and which are propositional
paths the kernel must witness (a patch is a path between worlds; a merge a pushout; a conflict a
witness that a square does not commute; admission by re-execution a definitional equality between
two derivations of the same world; a repair a path that stays inside the fibre of usable
candidates), and whether the higher structure Angiuli et al. use for patches extends to worlds,
plans and justifications or stops at the first level. Say where this reading makes a theorem we
have into an instance of a known one, and where it is only a metaphor. (2) The arithmetization
reading: admission by re-execution versus proof-carrying admission is the trade nano's charter made
(re-execute, macaroon-style) against breadstuffs' (a STARK per turn, light-client unfoolability).
What would arithmetizing obend's admission (the bidirectional checker on an annotated term, the law
judge on a write, the runner's `Legal` on an event) actually look like after what the zk languages
learned since snarky: which parts are circuit-shaped (the checker is syntax-directed and total; the
law judge is a closed Core4 term over Data; `Legal` is a fold over a log), which are not (search,
coinduction), what a receipt becomes (a compact witness of a path in the history groupoid), and
whether the disclosure dial gives a stranger a receipt they can check without the vault. (3) The
epistemic reading: authority as constructive knowledge (breadstuffs §0) and a slot's support as a
claim some member can witness; what distributed knowledge, common knowledge and the "cannot be
fooled" theorem become when there are several runners, several members and withdrawable
justifications, and whether that is the right logic for "distributed properties" (the Astra study
`astra/ASTRA-EFFECT-LAWS-20261031.md` runs concurrently on laws over effects; read it if it has landed
and say where the two meet).
